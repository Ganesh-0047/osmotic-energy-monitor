# 🌊 IoT-Enabled Osmotic Energy Generation System

## 💡 About the Project

Our project explores the generation of renewable electrical energy using the natural salinity difference between freshwater and saltwater.

The proposed system uses a suitable semipermeable membrane to create osmotic flow, which can be converted into mechanical energy through a turbine and then into electrical energy using a DC generator.

An ESP32-based IoT monitoring system measures important parameters such as voltage, current, power, pressure, temperature, TDS/salinity and water flow.

## ⚙️ Working Principle

Freshwater + Saltwater
        ↓
Salinity Gradient
        ↓
Osmotic Flow / Pressure
        ↓
Turbine
        ↓
DC Generator
        ↓
Electrical Energy
        ↓
Storage / LED Load

## 📊 IoT Monitoring

The ESP32-based system is designed to monitor:

- Voltage
- Current
- Power
- Energy
- Temperature
- TDS / Salinity
- Water flow
- Pressure

## 🚀 Future Improvements

- Replace the prototype RO membrane with a suitable FO/PRO membrane
- Add separate freshwater and saltwater chambers
- Improve turbine and generator efficiency
- Add energy storage
- Develop a complete real-time IoT dashboard
- Optimize the system for practical deployment

## 🎯 Goal

To explore freshwater–saltwater salinity gradients as a renewable energy resource and develop a low-cost prototype capable of converting the available hydraulic/mechanical energy into useful electrical energy.
